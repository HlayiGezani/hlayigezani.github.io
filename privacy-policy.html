Privacy Policy for Brasil Rádios OndaBR

Brasil Rádios OndaBR ("the App") is a live Brazilian radio streaming directory application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

1. Listener Data Collection (No Account Required)

Listeners do not need to create an account or sign in to use Brasil Rádios OndaBR. We do not collect, store, or sell personal listener information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise GPS location, contacts, or media files.

2. On-Device Local Storage

Your favorite radio stations, recent playback history, wake-up alarm settings, sleep timer preferences, read announcement status, and theme settings (Light/Dark mode) are stored locally on your device using Android's local SQLite database and preferences. You can clear this local data at any time directly inside the App's Settings screen or by uninstalling the App.

3. Station Announcements & Anonymous Broken Stream Reports

  • Public Station Announcements: The App synchronizes public radio stations and broadcast announcements (such as newly added radio stations) from Google Firebase Cloud Firestore. Receiving these announcements does not require a listener account and does not associate any personal identifier with your device.
  • Broken Stream Reports (Anonymous): If a radio streaming URL is offline or not working, you may optionally submit a "Report Broken Stream" alert to the Administrator. When you submit a report, only non-personal technical station details are transmitted to Firebase Cloud Firestore: the station ID, station name, stream URL, the selected issue reason (plus any optional note you type), and the timestamp. No user ID, email address, or personal identifier is attached to broken stream reports.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Public Radio Streams: When you tune in to a radio station, your device connects directly to that broadcaster's public audio stream URL. Standard network connection data (such as your IP address) is transmitted directly to the respective radio broadcaster's streaming server as part of normal internet audio playback.
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore & App Check): Used to deliver the public station catalog, deliver public station announcements, and receive anonymous broken stream reports.
  • Google AdMob (Advertising): If advertisements are displayed in the App via Google AdMob, Google may collect and use your device's Advertising ID, coarse IP-based location, and ad interaction diagnostics to serve and measure ads in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your Android device's Settings → Google → Ads menu.

5. Device Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to stream live radio audio, load station logos, sync the public station catalog and announcements, and send anonymous broken stream reports.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Used to keep live radio audio playing smoothly when your screen is off or while you use other apps, with visible Play/Pause media controls.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display the active media playback notification and alert you when the Administrator announces newly added stations.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Used to prevent the audio stream from cutting out while actively playing or triggering your scheduled wake-up radio alarm.

6. Administrator Account & Data Deletion

Google Sign-In inside the App is restricted exclusively to the authorized catalog Administrator for managing public radio stations, sending announcements, and resolving broken stream reports. An Administrator can sign out or permanently delete their Administrator account and associated cloud records at any time from the Admin Dashboard → Delete Admin Account button.

7. Broadcaster Copyright & Stream Removal (DMCA)

All radio station names, call signs, logos, and live audio broadcasts belong to their respective broadcasters and copyright owners. Brasil Rádios OndaBR operates as a directory of publicly available streams and does not host or modify copyrighted audio feeds. Broadcasters wishing to update station details or request immediate removal of a stream link may contact us at the email below.
